About Us

Waterloo's Home Comfort Crew, From Uptown to the Townships

From century homes off King Street near Wilfrid Laurier to the family subdivisions pushing out past Erbsville, our technicians keep Waterloo comfortable through January deep-freezes and the sticky July stretches that settle into the Grand River valley. Two universities, a busy tech corridor, and proper Mennonite country all in one Region, we know this housing stock because we heat and cool it every week.

Our Story

Two Universities, One Tech Hub, and Real Ontario Winters

At Waterloo Heating & Cooling, we started on King Street South in Uptown Waterloo, a few blocks from Wilfrid Laurier and a short walk from the tech offices that have reshaped this city over the past two decades. From that base we look after homes across Waterloo, Kitchener, Cambridge, and the surrounding townships, student rentals, family neighbourhoods, and rural properties alike.

Waterloo's housing tells a story you can read off the streets. The brick century homes around Mary-Allen and the Uptown core were built for a different era of heating, and they need furnaces, boilers, and ductwork matched to how the house actually breathes, not a generic install. Around the two universities the picture shifts again: converted houses with finished basement suites, ductless mini-splits and central air conditioners that get run hard through the school year, and townhouse rows near the tech corridor where the original HVAC is just starting to need real attention.

Drive twenty minutes in any direction and it changes again. Out in St. Jacobs, Elmira, Baden, New Hamburg, Breslau, and up toward Listowel, you'll find older farmhouses, rural properties off the gas grid leaning on propane and cold-climate heat pumps, and brand-new construction where ductless AC and smart thermostats are doing the heavy lifting. Working that full mix of housing, week in and week out, is what keeps our diagnostics sharp and our recommendations honest.

None of that changes how we approach the work. Before any wrench turns, we diagnose what's actually wrong, explain it in plain English, and lay out the options, repair versus replace, today's fix versus the one that keeps you from calling us again in February. We'd rather tell you a furnace has another five winters in it than sell you one it doesn't need, because in a Region this tightly connected, a straight answer is what keeps the phone ringing.

What Sets a Local Waterloo Crew Apart

When your furnace quits on the coldest night of the year or your AC can't keep up with a humid Grand River valley summer, you want a crew that already knows your part of the Region. We drive the same ION line and the same Conestoga Parkway you do, we've worked on the century homes near Uptown and the new builds out past Conestoga Mall, and we answer the phone like neighbours, not a distant dispatch queue.

Our Commitment

The Standard We Hold Every Job To

Every technician on the truck is licensed, insured, and trained on the high-efficiency furnaces, cold-climate heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, and smart thermostats that have become standard in newer Waterloo builds. We arrive in the window we promised, lay down drop sheets, wear boot covers, and clean up behind ourselves. Trusting someone with your furnace in January is a big enough ask on its own, you shouldn't have to mop up after us as well.

We work on every brand you'll find in a Waterloo Region basement, and we cover the full spectrum of home comfort: furnace installation and repair, central and ductless air conditioning, heat pump upgrades, fireplace installs, thermostat replacement, and thorough duct cleaning that clears out the dust and allergens your system has been pushing around all season. If a repair is genuinely outside what makes sense, we'll tell you straight, with the numbers behind it.
